Designed for staff and patients

From enquiry to human care.

A concept workflow that could be connected to a real clinic booking system after approval and secure integration.

1

Understand the question

The visitor selects a topic or asks about clinic services in English or Arabic.

2

Collect a visit preference

The demo helps select a service, doctor, date and preferred time window without inventing available slots.

3

Hand off safely

The receptionist would confirm availability using an authorized clinic system. Until then, visitors are directed to official channels.

When the Vercel OpenAI backend is available, free-text questions receive AI-generated answers. Guided service and sample appointment flows remain available offline. This independent demo is not connected to clinic records, calendars or staff; a production assistant requires clinic authorization, privacy safeguards and human review.
